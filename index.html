How Cycle Tracker Works

When does a new cycle start?

A new cycle begins on your first day of full bleeding (Light, Medium, or Heavy). Spotting alone does not start a new cycle.

What do the mucus options mean?

Each option covers both what you observe (see) and what you feel (sensation at the vulva). None/Dry means you feel dry AND see nothing. Cloudy/Damp means you see cloudy mucus OR feel damp/wet. Clear/Slippery means you see clear mucus OR feel slippery/lubricated. Log the option that matches either your observation or your feeling.

What counts as fertile mucus?

Both Clear/Slippery and Cloudy/Damp are considered fertile signs. Clear/Slippery indicates peak fertility, while Cloudy/Damp indicates potential fertility. None/Dry means no fertility signs are present.

How should I check my cervical mucus?

Check after 5pm for the most accurate reading. You can monitor mucus by paying attention to the sensation at the vulva throughout the day (feeling dry, wet, or lubricated), or by direct observation. For direct observation, wipe front-to-back with toilet tissue before using the bathroom, or use clean fingers (wash thoroughly first to maintain hygiene). Note what you see (cloudy, clear) and what you feel (dry, damp, slippery/stretchy).

Why am I still "Potentially Fertile" after my mucus dries up?

Cycle Tracker applies a 3-Day Rule. Your fertile window stays open for 3 full days after your last observation of fertile mucus, providing a safety margin.

When is pre-ovulation low fertility possible?

Before ovulation, low fertility requires 3 consecutive dry days after your period ends, with no fertile mucus in the last 5 days. The app also applies the Doering Rule, which uses your shortest past cycle to estimate the earliest possible ovulation. Days before that cutoff are considered potentially fertile even with dry mucus.

How is ovulation confirmed?

Cycle Tracker looks for a temperature shift: 3 consecutive days of temperatures that are at least 0.2°C higher than the highest of the previous 6 days. Once confirmed, you enter the Low Fertility (Luteal) phase.

What if I don't track my temperature (BBT)?

Cycle Tracker requires a temperature shift to confirm ovulation. Without temperature data, the app can still identify pre-ovulation low fertility through dry day rules, but you will not enter the confirmed post-ovulation phase without BBT data.
